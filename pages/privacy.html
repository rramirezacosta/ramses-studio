Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Ramses Studio is a desktop application for practicing music. It runs entirely on your own computer: stem separation, audio decoding, and the song library all happen locally. This policy explains what data the app and this website handle.

1. What the app stores on your device

The app keeps your imported songs, separated stems, and cover art in a local IndexedDB database inside your user profile. It also stores your preferences (theme, language, window state) locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly connect Google Drive.

2. Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect a Google account, the app backs up your library to the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). Only you can access that space. OAuth sign-in runs in the app's main process; tokens are stored encrypted by your operating system (Electron safeStorage) and are never shown to the renderer or sent to any Ramses server. You can disconnect or delete the app data at any time from Google Drive's "Manage apps" page.

3. Network requests the app makes

  • YouTube imports — when you import from a YouTube URL, the app downloads the audio from YouTube using yt-dlp. This is a direct request from your machine to YouTube.
  • Google Drive — only if you connect an account, and only to your own Drive.
  • Update checks — none are performed automatically.

There is no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind. The app has no backend of its own.

4. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser, referrer) as part of operating the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

5. Your rights

Because all data lives on your device (and, optionally, your own Google Drive), you control it completely: delete the app, clear its storage, or revoke its Drive access and everything is gone. There is no Ramses account to delete.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/rramirezacosta/ramses-studio/issues.
