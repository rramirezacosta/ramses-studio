Everything you need to rehearse

From raw recording to loop-able practice stems, entirely on your machine.

🎛️

Six-stem separation

Bundled AI model that splits any track into drums, bass, other, vocals, guitar, and piano — running locally on your machine, no cloud required.

🎚️

DAW-style timeline

Load separated stems into track lanes with waveforms. Mute, solo, pan, and mix each instrument independently.

🔁

Section looping

Drag over any waveform to grab a loop of the passage you are rehearsing and keep it spinning.

📥

Any source, decoded

Bundled FFmpeg decodes WAV, FLAC, and MP3 imports; yt-dlp integration pulls audio straight from YouTube.

🔒

Local-first by design

Inference, songs, and generated stems never leave your machine. Your library lives in your home folder.

Practice in four steps

From import to hands-on-keys in under a minute.

1

Import audio — choose a WAV, FLAC, or MP3 file, or paste a YouTube URL.

2

Separate — the bundled AI model runs locally.

3

Open the song — all six stems load as tracks in the timeline.

4

Rehearse — mute and solo tracks, balance the mix, and loop the tricky sections.
